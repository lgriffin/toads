# toads hub guides

How to use [toads.gg](https://toads.gg), written for players. Pick the guide for what you are in the guild:

| You are | Read | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not in the guild yet, or a social member | [Visitors and applicants](visitors.md) | The public pages, logging in with Discord, applying to raid, your interview room |
| A trial or raider | [Raiders](raiders.md) | Your first sign-in, Me, claims, absences, the Raid page, Logs, the bank, highlights |
| An officer of a raid night, or a global officer | [Officers](officers.md) | Your night's officer section, claims, recruitment, comps, loot, strategy, absences, the bank desk |
| A super admin | [Super admins](super-admins.md) | Officer grants, badges from Discord roles, bank grants and tokens, View as |

Each guide builds on the one before it: officers can do everything raiders can, and super admins everything officers
can. The **What You Can Do** page in your account menu (`/features`) lists what your own role opens, live.

Your role on the hub comes from your Discord roles in the toads server. If something you expect is missing, check your
Discord roles first, then ask an officer of your raid night.

## For whoever keeps these up to date

These guides describe what a player sees and clicks. The rules behind each page are in the docs next to the code
(`docs/*.md`), and setup and configuration are in [ADMIN_GUIDE.md](../../ADMIN_GUIDE.md). When a pull request changes a
page, a menu or who may use something, update the guide it touches in the same pull request. Copy rules: the guild is
lowercase "toads", and the wording is toads and ponds only.

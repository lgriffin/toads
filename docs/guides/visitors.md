# Guide for visitors and applicants

Welcome to the pond. This guide is for anyone looking at [toads.gg](https://toads.gg) from outside the guild, and for
anyone who wants to raid with us.

## What you can see without logging in

The top bar holds the public pages. None of them needs an account.

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Who we are, our progression and fastest clears, and the **Recruitment** section: pick a night and see which tanks, healers and damage specs that night is looking for. The "Join us" button jumps straight to it. |
| **How We Raid** | What we expect on raid night: prep, flasks and consumes, speed, the Loot Council and how we measure ourselves. |
| **Hub Features** | Everything the hub does for everyone, for raiders and for officers. Signed out, the raider and officer columns are dimmed so you can see what joining gets you. |
| **Story** | The guild's story. |
| **Recruit** | The application form. |

We raid on three nights, Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday, and each night is its own team with its own officers.

### Reading the recruitment needs

On the homepage, pick the night you are interested in. Each group (Tanks, Healers, Damage) lists the specs that night's
officers want, with the most wanted marked. **Full for now** means that group has nothing listed for the night. Strong
players of any class are always welcome to apply, even to a full night.

### Just want to be social?

You do not need to apply. Poke any member of toads in game for a social invite and come and sit in the pond.

## Applying to raid

Applying takes three steps, and the **Recruit** page walks you through them.

1. **Join our Discord.** Your interview happens there, so you must be in the toads server before you send your
   application. The Recruit page has the invite link.
2. **Log in with Discord.** Press **Log In with Discord** in the top bar or on the Recruit page. We use your Discord
   account to find you in the server. We never see your email or password. If you started the form before logging
   in, your answers are kept in that browser tab while you sign in.
3. **Answer the questions.** Your character name, your class, the role you want to play and an optional second role,
   why toads, and which night you want to raid. We ask for no age, email or real name.

The "What we need right now" card beside the form lists the same needs as the homepage, with the night each is for.

### Your interview room

As soon as you send the application, the Toad Bot opens a private channel for you in Discord, called
`#interview-<your character>`. You, the bot and that night's interview panel are in it, and the bot posts your answers
there. The Recruit page shows **Open your interview room** once it exists: press it to jump straight into the channel.

Only the officers in your room read your answers.

If the page says your room did not open, it also says why. The usual reason is that you left the Discord server; join
again and an officer can reopen the room for you. If it says the bot is missing a permission, an officer has to fix
that, so mention it in Discord.

### What happens next

The night's officers talk to you in your room. When they decide:

- **Accepted:** the Toad Bot gives you that night's raider role in Discord and posts a welcome in your room. The next
  time you log in to the hub you are a raider, so read the [raiders' guide](raiders.md). Inviting you to the guild in
  game is still done by an officer.
- **Declined:** the bot removes the interview room and the Recruit page says your application was declined. You can
  apply again after 30 days.

## Logged in but not on a raid team?

If you log in with Discord but hold no raid night role yet, you can still browse the guild's raids and the guild bank,
and apply. Pages for a raid team, such as Raid, say you are not on a raid team yet. That changes once an officer
accepts you or gives you a raid night role in Discord.

## Getting help

Ask in the toads Discord. Officers can see your application and its interview room, so there is no need to send your
answers again.
